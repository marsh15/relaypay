# RelayPay v2.0.0-m17 (draft): webhooks as evidence and provider chaos

This is a draft. It covers only the commits that have landed on `main`. It is not the v2.0 release,
and it claims no release evidence, no measured numbers, and no completed milestone. M17 is still in
progress. The plan is [docs/plans/v2.0-never-loses-a-paisa.md](../plans/v2.0-never-loses-a-paisa.md).

## Behaviour changes

- **Merchant webhook retries.** At most 8 attempts, then `DEAD_LETTER`. Retry *n* waits a random
  delay in `[0, min(3600, 5 * 2^(n-1))]` seconds. The reference gives the schedule in full.
- **Inbound PSP webhooks.** Accepted at `POST /api/inbound/v1/psp/{provider}`, verified over the raw
  bytes with `PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, and stored once per provider event id. Only `SIMULATOR` is
  routed. Events are applied by the poller task `apply_psp_events`.
- **Recovery review budget.** Only `LOOKUP` attempts count toward `PROVIDER_INDETERMINATE`.
  Mutation and webhook evidence attempts no longer consume the five-lookup budget (`8598872`).
- **Provider simulator.** A chaos profile decides each stable key's first mutation from a seed. The
  profile can add errors, slow responses, lost responses, pending and declined outcomes, and
  webhook drop, duplicate and delay. Pushes are signed, at least once, and retried.
- **New settings.** `PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (production rejects its development default),
  `PSP_WEBHOOK_REPLAY_SECONDS`, `PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_URL`, and `PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_PUSH_SECONDS`. See
  [configuration](../configuration.md).

## What M17 does not change

- `/api/v1` is unchanged by these commits.
- Financial finalization still uses the shared finalizer. A PSP webhook can reach the ledger only
  through that finalizer; the lookup review-budget change is described above.
- Operations resolve by polling, as before M17. A PSP event that is `DEFERRED` changes nothing.

## Not yet in M17

These items are out of scope for M17 or not landed. They come from the handoff's section 7 and the
M16 follow-ups.

- UPI collect and `requires_action`, including `pending_deadline_at` in provider-operation recovery,
  and UPI and card flavours in the simulator.
- Merchant events v2: relaxing `merchant_events` parents, `payout.succeeded.v1`, and the void, expiry
  and cancel events. M16's notes had moved the void, expiry and cancel events into M17, and the
  handoff now places them outside M17.
- Feeding provider webhook chaos into the chaos harness, and the three-times webhook replay harness.
- Wiring the Razorpay and Cashfree adapters into the inbox (operation matching and per-provider
  secrets).
- The ordering decision, `packages/relaypay/psp/ordering.py::decide`. It is hand-written by the
  maintainer, so PSP events stay `DEFERRED` until it lands.
- M18 in-database reconciliation at scale: COPY import, chunked matching, statement fee and tax
  columns, daily auto-import, and the console upload endpoint.
- GCRA rate limiting, payout-locking wiring, `balance_version`, and the locking benchmark.
- Migrations, indexes, or hot-path fixes that come from load-test or query-plan findings.
- Cancel before authorization, refund fee reversal, and creating fee-schedule versions. The
  fee-schedule admin read API is also not landed.
- The final v2.0 release: evidence JSON, README numbers and the drift check, release notes, the blog,
  and the full chaos run.
- The maintainer's hand-written modules: the invariant checker (`proof/invariants.py`), the payout
  locking strategies, the fee calculator, and the PSP ordering decision. Until the invariant checker
  lands, the chaos run reports counters and harness failures only.
