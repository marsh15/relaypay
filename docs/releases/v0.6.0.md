# RelayPay v0.6.0 — Versioned connectors and synthetic commerce

RelayPay v0.6.0 extracts common connector contracts from the green payment and bank adapters,
adds encrypted credential rotation and durable connector health, accepts signed inbound webhook
evidence, and synchronizes a narrowly scoped synthetic commerce order model.

> **Synthetic data only.** Never submit real credentials, bank, card, identity, customer, or
> regulated data. This release is not deployed or hosted.

## Added

- Concrete payment and bank adapters behind common request, lookup, validation, classification,
  timeout, rate-limit, and health contracts.
- Immutable connector credential versions encrypted at rest. New secrets are shown once; pending
  versions must be verified before explicit activation revokes the prior active version.
- PostgreSQL-authoritative circuit state, health observations, provider rate-limit evidence, and
  normalized `TEMPORARY`, `PERMANENT`, `AMBIGUOUS`, and `RATE_LIMITED` errors.
- Exact-byte inbound webhook retention with signature-before-processing, timestamp replay windows,
  immutable digests, provider-event deduplication, reclaimable leases, processing attempts, and
  dead-letter state.
- An independently credentialed commerce database for orders, payment links, total reconciliation,
  and paid/refunded synchronization. Inventory and distributed sagas remain excluded.

## Compatibility and migration

Existing `/api/v1` payment contracts and evidence bytes are unchanged. Read the
[v0.6.0 migration guide](https://github.com/marsh15/relaypay/blob/v0.6.0/docs/migrations/v0.6.0.md).

## Runnable proof

```bash
make connector-demo
```

The proof creates and verifies a synthetic connector version, activates it, authenticates and
deduplicates exact inbound bytes, and synchronizes a linked commerce order to `PAID`.

## Limitations

- Connector verification is synchronous and administrator initiated.
- The commerce service models only orders and payment links.
- Redis may accelerate workers but never supplies durable connector, webhook, or commerce truth.
- No real networks, credentials, inventory, deployment, hosting, or production certification.
